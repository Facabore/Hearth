## How to read this

Every finding is tagged with how sure it is:

- **Verified**: reproduced in the running app, with a screenshot or a measurement, and where it matters the line of code.
- **Hypothesis**: the behavior is verified, but whether it hurts real people needs data this audit does not have.
- **Opportunity**: no defect; something worth exploring.

Priority weighs impact × frequency × severity × confidence in the evidence, against effort. P1 = fix next; P2 = plan it; P3 = when convenient.

## 1. Executive summary

The core loops work and are fast. Settling a debt takes two taps and ends in a visible "Pago registrado" with the new state. A shared expense preselects the split by plan and shows its impact on the plan before saving. Every stacked screen has a way back. No screen but one scrolls sideways, and the screens settle in about a second locally.

The weak points are about **trust in the numbers and in what the app lets you do**, more than about looks:

1. A **viewer can fill in a whole expense** and only learns at the end that she cannot save it.
2. The record form **defaults the paying account to the first one alphabetically** ("Ahorros"), not the one the person uses, so personal balances drift without anyone noticing.
3. **Personal shows two different answers** to "how much did I spend this month": 163,000 on Inicio and 1,383,000 under Plan › Mi consumo.
4. Once a plan line passes 90 %, **every expense in that category asks "¿Guardar de todos modos?"**, and the confirm button lands under the bottom bar.
5. The household vocabulary and layout fixes the owner approved on 2026-10-23 (U1, U2, U3, U5, U8, U10) **never reached the code and have no issue on the board**. The app still says "Liquidar", "Capacidad", "Línea", "Reinicia · Acumula · Ajusta", "Asignado · Aportó · Consumió".

Fixing items 1–4 is mostly small work with a high payoff in trust. Item 5 is a planning gap: decisions exist, delivery does not.

## 3. Findings

Ordered by priority. Fields follow the audit brief: problem, who, context, evidence, root cause (certainty), consequence, solution, priority, effort, metric, validation.

### F1. A viewer fills in a whole expense and is refused at the end · P1 · Verified

- **Problem:** VIEWER members see the + button and the full record form. Sofi typed an amount, picked a category, confirmed the plan warning, and only then read "No tienes permiso para hacer esto en este espacio."
- **Who:** every VIEWER of a shared space (a teenager, a relative who only follows the household).
- **Context:** the most frequent job, recording an expense, in a shared space.
- **Evidence:** [viewer-denied-after-filling](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/viewer-denied-after-filling.png); Sofi's Hogar bar shows the same + as Ana's. The members, planner and capacity screens do check for `VIEWER`; the record form and the space shell do not mention it.
- **Root cause (high certainty):** the FAB and the record route do not read the member's role; only the API enforces it. It breaks the Batch 2 rule "Roles hide the controls the user cannot use".
- **Consequence:** wasted effort and a sense of being blocked by a bug. For the household, a viewer may think an expense was recorded when it was not.
- **Solution:** hide the + in a space where the person cannot record, or replace it with a short sheet "En Hogar eres lector: puedes ver, no registrar". Guard the `/new` route the same way, so a deep link explains instead of failing.
- **Effort:** low. The role is already in the shell's data.
- **Metric:** 0 permission errors (`Forbidden` codes) on expense writes from the UI.
- **Validation:** a component test with a VIEWER (no + in the bar; `/new` shows the explanation), and the e2e role scenario.

### F2. The paying account defaults to "Ahorros", not to the account the person uses · P1 · Verified

- **Problem:** in Personal and in shared spaces, the form says "Pagas tú desde Ahorros (tu cuenta personal)" or "Cuenta: Ahorros" by default. Ana recorded every expense from Bancolombia, and the form still offers Ahorros, a savings account.
- **Who:** everyone, on every expense. Strongest for new people, whose sign-up opens five starter accounts.
- **Context:** recording at the moment of paying, fast, often without reading the secondary rows.
- **Evidence:** at `b8c2b31`, `src/modules/ledger/ui/record/record-form.tsx:268` set `accounts[0]?.id`. The accounts come sorted by name, so "Ahorros" wins. Screenshots in flows A, D and E.
- **Root cause (high certainty):** the default is positional, not behavioral.
- **Consequence:** account balances silently go wrong (savings drained on paper, the real bank too high). That undermines the product's promise "each member knows their real cash out", and the error surfaces weeks later, far from its cause.
- **Solution:** default to the account this person used last in this space (or last overall in Personal), never a SAVINGS account unless it was the last one. Keep the row visible above "Más opciones".
- **Effort:** low to medium. It needs "last used" read from recent movements or stored as a preference; the uncertainty is where to keep it.
- **Metric:** share of saved expenses where the person changed the account from the default (target under 10 %); edits to the account field after saving.
- **Validation:** instrument both rates before and after; a test that the second expense defaults to the first one's account.

### F3. Personal gives two different answers to "how much did I spend?" · P1 · Verified

- **Problem:** Ana's Inicio says "Gastado en octubre $ 163.000" (personal movements only). Plan › Mi consumo says "Consumo $ 1.383.000" (personal plus her shares in Hogar and Viaje). Nothing on Inicio says the first number leaves out the shared spaces, and Inicio has no way to the second.
- **Who:** every person in at least one shared space, which is the household audience the product puts first.
- **Context:** opening the app to check the month.
- **Evidence:** [personal-home](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/personal-home.png), [personal-my-spending](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/personal-my-spending.png). The figures add up: 163,000 personal + 480,000 rent share + 360,000 market share + 160,000 utilities share + 40,000 cinema + 180,000 trip = 1,383,000.
- **Root cause (high certainty):** U4 ("Tu mes en todos tus espacios" on Inicio) and U10 (one hero figure) were decided but not built.
- **Consequence:** the headline figure of Personal underreports spending by 88 % for Ana. People act on the first number they see; this one says the month is fine. It is also the clearest breach of "Personal first".
- **Solution:** apply U4: on Personal Inicio, under the headline, one line "Con tus espacios compartidos: $ 1.383.000 ›" opening Mi consumo. Rename the headline to say what it counts ("Gastado aquí en octubre"). Apply U10 in Mi consumo.
- **Effort:** low to medium. The unified view endpoint already exists (`/api/v1/me/unified-view`); the cost is one extra read on Inicio.
- **Metric:** taps from opening the app to the unified figure (today 2, target 0 to read, 1 to drill in); visits to Mi consumo.
- **Validation:** 5-person task test: "¿Cuánto gastaste este mes en total?" Count right answers and time.

### F4. The 90 % line warning interrupts the frequent job and hides its own button · P1 · Verified

- **Problem:** from 90 % of a line, "Guardar gasto" turns into a second step "¿Guardar de todos modos?". On a 390×844 phone, its confirm button lands under the bottom bar, so the person has to scroll to find it. Each later expense in that category asks again for the rest of the month.
- **Who:** households with an active plan, late in the month, exactly when lines fill up.
- **Context:** recording at the till, one-handed.
- **Evidence:** [line-warning-confirm-under-nav](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/line-warning-confirm-under-nav.png); the behavior is specified in `tests/e2e/features/line-warning.feature` (#142). The copy shows "96,59 %", two decimals of false precision.
- **Root cause (medium certainty):** a deliberate safety step (#142) built as a blocking confirmation, and the form's content plus the fixed bottom bar push it out of view. It contradicts a pattern the Batch 2 review praised: "A negative fund balance warns after saving instead of blocking."
- **Consequence:** friction on the most frequent job, warning fatigue (people learn to tap through), and a real risk of abandoning the record.
- **Solution:** for 90–100 %, save at once and say it in the toast ("Mercado y casa queda en 97 %, te quedan $ 50.000"). Ask first only when the expense goes over the plan. Round the percentage to whole numbers. Hide the bottom bar on the record form, or keep the primary action sticky above it.
- **Effort:** low. Copy, one threshold, and layout. It changes #142's agreed behavior, so it needs the owner.
- **Metric:** confirm rate of the warning (if it is near 100 %, the step adds nothing), time to save, record abandonment after the warning shows.
- **Validation:** instrument "warning shown / confirmed / abandoned" before changing anything.

### F5. Approved household vocabulary and layout fixes never shipped · P1 · Verified

- **Problem:** the shipped app still uses the model's words that the owner replaced on 2026-10-23, and the layouts those decisions fixed.
- **Who:** every household member; strongest on the planner and dashboards.
- **Evidence:**

  | Decision | Decided | Shipped |
  |---|---|---|
  | U1 vocabulary | "Cuánto aporta cada uno", "Rubro", "Por repartir", "según aportes", "Empieza de cero" | "Capacidad total", "Líneas", "Sin asignar $ 0", "Según capacidad", "Reinicia · Acumula · Ajusta", "Asignado · Aportó · Consumió · Diferencia" ([plan-lines](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/plan-lines.png), [line-editor](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/line-editor.png)) |
  | U2 one verb | "Saldar" everywhere | "Liquidar" on suggestions, "Registrar pago" on the list, plus the apology "Liquidar solo prellena el formulario." (`es.json:986`, `:1011`) |
  | U3 view order | Rubros · Por grupo · Miembros · Fondo · Tendencia | Líneas · Miembros · Fondo · Por grupo · Tendencia; Personal still Mi consumo · Próximos · Por grupo · Tendencia · Líneas |
  | U5 line editor | rollover and allocation under "Más opciones" | everything on one 1,336 px screen |
  | U8 Ajustes | three summary rows | 1,503 px long ([settings](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/settings.png)) |
  | U10 one hero | "Lo que gastaste tú" | two equal heroes, "Consumo" and "Salidas" |

  `es.json` holds none of "Rubro", "Saldar", "Por repartir", "Cuánto aporta". No GitHub issue tracks U1–U12.
- **Root cause (high certainty):** the decisions were recorded in docs and Penpot, but no issue carried them to the board, so delivery never picked them up.
- **Consequence:** people have to decode budget-engine terms; the critique's own example, "Ana does not think 'my capacity is 1,600,000'". Two verbs for one action keep confusing settle-up.
- **Solution:** open one issue per decision group (copy U1+U2, layout U3+U5+U8+U10) on project 4, and ship the copy first: it is i18n-only.
- **Effort:** low for copy (es and en catalogs), medium for the layouts.
- **Metric:** task success on "¿Cuánto pone Juan este mes?" and "Salda tu deuda"; questions about terms in a test.
- **Validation:** first-click and comprehension test of the planner with 5 people, before and after the copy.

### F6. Tendencia overflows the page sideways · P2 · Verified

- **Problem:** at 390 px the document is 515 px wide: the period table pushes the whole page sideways, and its last column is cut ("agosto 2"). With one period of history, the chart is an empty grid with four dots.
- **Who:** anyone opening Plan › Tendencia, which in the first months is everyone.
- **Evidence:** [trend-overflow](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/trend-overflow.png); measured `scrollWidth 515 > clientWidth 390`. AGENTS.md: "No horizontal scroll at 390 or 320 px."
- **Root cause (high certainty):** the table is not inside its own scroll container, or has a min width wider than the screen.
- **Consequence:** a broken-looking screen; on touch, the page wobbles sideways while scrolling.
- **Solution:** contain the table (its own `overflow-x: auto` with a visible cue), or show one card per line on phones. With fewer than two periods, replace the chart with "La tendencia aparece desde el segundo mes".
- **Effort:** low.
- **Metric:** the layout gate at 390 and 320 passes for Tendencia.
- **Validation:** add Tendencia, with a single period, to `tests/e2e/layout.spec.ts`.

### F7. Plan › Miembros is a 2,567 px wall of numbers · P2 · Verified

- **Problem:** 4 lines × 3 members × 4 rows = 48 money rows on one phone screen, including a viewer with "$ 0" on every row.
- **Who:** the person who manages the plan, asking "who is behind?"
- **Evidence:** [plan-members-2567px](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/plan-members-2567px.png).
- **Root cause (medium certainty):** the API's member × line matrix is rendered as is, line by line, instead of answering the question.
- **Consequence:** the answer ("Juan is 482,000 behind on Mercado y casa") is buried; people stop opening the view.
- **Solution:** lead with one row per member, their total gap and its direction (for example "Juan: le faltan $ 482.000 en Mercado y casa"), and expand per line on tap. Leave out members whose every figure is zero.
- **Effort:** medium.
- **Metric:** time to answer "¿Quién va atrasado y en qué?"
- **Validation:** the same task test as F5.

### F8. Status badges and "Gastado" misread planned money · P2 · Verified

- **Problem:** Arriendo, paid exactly as planned (1,200,000 of 1,200,000), carries the warning badge "Al límite". The Ahorro line shows "Gastado $ 200.000" for a transfer into savings, and the plan headline "Gastado $ 2.780.000 de $ 4.000.000" counts that saving as spending.
- **Who:** household planners reading the month.
- **Evidence:** [plan-lines](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/plan-lines.png). `es.json:1327` maps `HIGH` to "Al límite".
- **Root cause (medium certainty):** one badge scale for every funding type; a fully used fixed bill is not a risk, and saving is not spending.
- **Consequence:** false alarms on fixed bills; saving looks like overspending, which nudges people to save less.
- **Solution:** for a line with a single expected payment (rent), "Pagado" in a neutral or positive tone; for SAVINGS lines, "Ahorrado" instead of "Gastado", and keep savings out of the "Gastado" headline (show it apart: "Ahorrado $ 200.000 de $ 600.000").
- **Effort:** low to medium. Copy plus a rule per funding type; the API already returns the funding.
- **Metric:** comprehension in the planner test.
- **Validation:** ask "¿Cómo va el arriendo este mes?" and note whether people read a problem.

### F9. A dirty form is discarded without asking · P2 · Verified

- **Problem:** with 55,000 typed in the record form, Volver goes straight back to Inicio and the amount is gone.
- **Who:** everyone, especially on phones where the back gesture is easy to trigger.
- **Evidence:** flow B: the URL changed to the space home with no dialog. Batch 2 rubric R1: "A dirty form asks before discarding."
- **Root cause (high certainty):** no dirty-state guard on the record route.
- **Consequence:** lost entries, and people re-entering expenses later from memory, with worse data.
- **Solution:** if something was typed, ask "¿Descartar este gasto?" with "Descartar" and "Seguir editando", for Volver and the system back.
- **Effort:** low to medium (the router's blocker plus the system back).
- **Metric:** records started vs saved.
- **Validation:** a component test for both paths.

### F10. Inicio's recent list shows a time but no date · P2 · Verified

- **Problem:** Recientes shows "23:45 · Mercado" for entries from October 3, 12 and 20; nothing says they are not from today. Movimientos groups by date correctly.
- **Who:** everyone glancing at Inicio.
- **Evidence:** [personal-home](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/personal-home.png). (The identical times come from the seeding, which sent dates without times; the missing date is the UI's.)
- **Root cause (high certainty):** the row's meta line prints the time only.
- **Consequence:** people misjudge what happened today, and may record duplicates.
- **Solution:** "Hoy · 14:32", "Ayer", then "3 oct".
- **Effort:** low. The formatting must go through `src/shared/ui/format/` (D23).
- **Metric:** duplicate expenses (same amount and category within a day).
- **Validation:** a component test with yesterday's and last week's entries.

### F11. Members who never take part show up on every money screen · P2 · Hypothesis

- **Problem:** Sofi, a viewer who never pays or consumes, appears as a payer and receiver chip in "Registrar pago", as "Sofi $ 0 · 0,00 %" in the capacities, as a row of zeros in every line's allocation and in the member matrix, in the Movimientos filters, and as "a paz y salvo" in Saldos.
- **Who:** households with viewers; trips where someone joins only to follow.
- **Evidence:** [settle-form](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/settle-form.png), the planner, [plan-members-2567px](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/plan-members-2567px.png).
- **Root cause (medium certainty):** member lists come from membership, not from participation.
- **Consequence:** noise on dense screens, and the possibility of picking a viewer by mistake as the payer of a settlement.
- **Solution (needs a product decision):** leave out members who cannot record and have no money in the period, or put them behind "Mostrar todos".
- **Effort:** medium; it touches several screens.
- **Metric:** wrong-member corrections on settlements.
- **Validation:** decide first whether a viewer can carry a capacity at all.

### F12. Link invitations stay open for several people, ADMIN included · P2 · Hypothesis

- **Problem:** after Juan and Sofi joined, Miembros still lists their links as "Enlace de invitación · Administrador · varias personas · vence el 4/11/2026" and "Lector · varias personas". Anyone who gets the ADMIN link in the next week joins as an administrator.
- **Who:** owners who share a link in a group chat.
- **Evidence:** [members](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/members.png).
- **Root cause (medium certainty):** multi-use links are the design (HU-E1-06); the screen gives no nudge to revoke them once used, and ADMIN is offered for multi-use links.
- **Consequence:** over-granted roles in a money app. Small likelihood, high impact.
- **Solution (needs a decision):** make ADMIN invitations single use, or show "Lo usaron 1 persona · Revocar" next to each link.
- **Effort:** low for the label; medium if the rule changes (it would be a contract change).
- **Metric:** active ADMIN links older than one day.
- **Validation:** check with the owner how links are shared in practice.

### F13. Settling up never touches the accounts by default · P2 · Hypothesis

- **Problem:** "Sale de (opcional)" defaults to "Ninguna", among seven account chips that include the five starter accounts. A settlement saved with the default moves no money in Juan's own accounts.
- **Who:** members who also use Hearth as their personal manager.
- **Evidence:** [settle-form](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/settle-form.png).
- **Root cause (low to medium certainty):** "optional" keeps settle-up fast, at the cost of the personal picture.
- **Consequence:** personal balances drift after each settlement, as in F2.
- **Solution:** default to the person's last used account, keep "Ninguna" as a choice, and show only accounts in use.
- **Effort:** low, with the same "last used" source as F2.
- **Metric:** share of settlements saved with an account.
- **Validation:** with the F2 instrumentation.

### F14. First use has no next step · P2 · Hypothesis

- **Problem:** Carla lands on "Gastado en octubre $ 0", five starter accounts at $ 0 (scrolling sideways), and "Aún no hay movimientos. Registra el primero con +." Nothing invites her to set her real balances, create a plan, or start a shared space, though sharing is the product's distinctive value.
- **Who:** every new person.
- **Evidence:** [first-use-home](assets/ux-audit-2026-10-28/first-use-home.png).
- **Root cause (medium certainty):** the starter accounts (issue 169) solved "no account to record from" without opening balances.
- **Consequence:** balances start wrong (everything from 0), and people who came for the household may not find how to share. No retention data exists to confirm the impact.
- **Solution:** a dismissible three-step card: "¿Cuánto tienes hoy en tus cuentas?", "Registra tu primer gasto", "Comparte con tu hogar".
- **Effort:** medium.
- **Metric:** share of new people who set an opening balance in week 1; share who create or join a shared space in week 1.
- **Validation:** onboarding test with 5 first-time people; then cohort numbers once real usage exists.

### F15. Ajustes: misleading copy and a long scroll · P3 · Verified

### F16. The line editor's controls have no labels · P3 · Verified

### F17. "Dividir con alguien" sits behind "Más opciones" · P3 · Verified

### F18. Regional words in the base Spanish catalog · P3 · Verified

- **Problem:** "a paz y salvo" and "Estás a paz y salvo" live in the base `es.json` (`:982`, `:984`, `:992`). AGENTS.md: regional words belong to the regional variants of D24 (#180), not to the base catalog.

### F19. Small copy and format details · P3 · Verified

- After several sign-ins in a few minutes, the throttle says "Demasiados intentos. Espera unos minutos". Say how long.
